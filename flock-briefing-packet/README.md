## How to use this

**If you're a resident:** Read `packet.md` end-to-end. It's structured so you can read it cold and walk into a city council meeting an hour later. The "What to do this week" section gives you three concrete actions sized to whatever time you have.

**If you're a journalist:** Every factual claim is sourced at the bottom. Use the timeline as a starting point and verify against the originals.

**If you're an advocate or organizer:** Fork the packet, swap in your city's name and local examples, and use the FOIA template (state-citation list inside) to start digging. Tell us what you find via an Issue.

**If you're a council member or PD official:** The questions in the council script are the ones residents will start asking. Better to have answers ready than to be caught flat-footed.
