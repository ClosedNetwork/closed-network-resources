# Contributing to Closed Network Resources

Thanks for showing up. This repo is meant to be useful, current, and accurate — and that only happens if people who know things keep nudging it forward.

## The fastest, lowest-friction ways to contribute

### 1. Open an Issue (3 minutes)

We've set up Issue templates for the most common contributions:

- **Add a city vote** — your town voted on Flock or another surveillance contract and we should add it to the timeline.
- **Broken or outdated source** — a link is dead, a number is stale, or a story has been corrected.
- **Translation request** — you want to help translate a packet into another language.

Pick a template, fill it in, hit submit. We'll handle the rest.

### 2. Submit a Pull Request

If you're comfortable with Git:

1. Fork the repo.
2. Create a branch named for what you're doing (`add-portland-vote`, `fix-foia-template-illinois`).
3. Make your edits in Markdown. Keep the existing voice — calm, sourced, plain English.
4. Open a PR. Explain what you changed and why. Cite a source for any factual change.

Style notes:
- Every factual claim needs a public source.
- Prefer primary sources (court records, official city statements, audit logs) over secondary reporting where possible.
- Don't use scare language. The receipts speak for themselves.
- Keep it accessible — if a 16-year-old or a 70-year-old can't read it, rewrite it.

### 3. Adapt and re-share

You don't need to contribute back. The CC BY 4.0 license means you can lift the whole packet, swap "Sedona" for your city's name, change the brand to your local advocacy group, and walk it into your own city council meeting tomorrow. That's the whole point.

If you do, drop us a line — we'd love to feature wins on the show.

## What we won't accept

- Calls for vandalism, sabotage, or any illegal action. The packets stay on solid legal ground.
- Unsourced claims, no matter how true they feel.
- Partisan framing that makes the materials harder to hand to a politically mixed council. The arguments work across the political spectrum and we want them to keep working.

## Code of conduct

Be decent. Disagree with the work, not the person. We reserve the right to close threads or block contributors who can't manage that.
